# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective clients and existing partners use MSM.digital to understand the agency's specialist expertise, inspect relevant work, meet the responsible leaders, and decide whether to start a conversation. MSM editors manage the site through the shared Sanity Studio.

## Product Purpose

MSM.digital presents four specialist business units that together cover the modern customer journey. Success means visitors can quickly identify the relevant Unit, verify its expertise through real Cases and People, and contact MSM.

## Positioning

MSM combines four distinct specialist Units inside one agency: Communications, Channel Marketing, XR Labs, and Technology Systems. Each Unit has its own expertise and leadership while contributing to one integrated customer-journey offering.

## Operating Context

The MSM website is one frontend in a multisite platform backed by one Sanity dataset. Cases and People are systemwide content; MSM Units reference them without adding MSM-specific fields to those shared documents.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Each MSM Unit has an overview entry and a dedicated public Unit page.
- A Unit may reference multiple Cases and People; the same Case or Person may belong to several Units.
- Unit attribution and leadership are owned by the MSM Unit. Shared Case and Person documents remain unchanged.
- Unit pages present identity, claim, narrative, capabilities, leaders, attributed Cases, and a contact action.
- English launches first. German variants are added later and must not be represented by empty or silently duplicated English documents.
- Existing MSM routing, accessibility, performance, and multisite content boundaries must remain intact.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the incumbent MSM.digital identity and frontend design system.
- Units do not currently have individual logos. The animated MSM cube mark represents them until optional Unit marks exist.
- The canonical Unit names are Communications, Channel Marketing, XR Labs, and Technology Systems. "AR, VR, MR & AI Glasses" is an XR Labs descriptor, not part of its name.
- MSM surfaces are angular and do not use rounded-card styling.

## Evidence on Hand

- Approved English overview and Unit copy for all four Units.
- Existing MSM.digital Unit imagery may be used as temporary hero media.
- The Sanity development dataset contains published MSM Cases and a shared English Person pool.
- Case and leadership assignments are intentionally editorial; no assignments may be fabricated from names or old-site content.

## Product Principles

- Make specialization immediately understandable without fragmenting the MSM master brand.
- Prove expertise with real work and real leaders rather than unsupported claims.
- Keep MSM-specific relationships out of shared Case and Person documents.
- Preserve one authoritative editorial source for every relationship.
- Extend the incumbent system instead of introducing a competing visual language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Unit navigation, media, motion, filters, and calls to action must remain keyboard accessible, screen-reader legible, and usable with reduced-motion preferences.
