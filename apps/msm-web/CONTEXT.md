# MSM Website

The MSM website presents MSM.digital's specialist structure while consuming selected systemwide content from the shared platform.

## Language

**MSM Unit**:
A specialist business unit within MSM.digital with its own expertise, leadership, and body of work.
_Avoid_: Global Unit, subunit

**Unit Attribution**:
An MSM-owned association between an MSM Unit and a Shared Case. Several MSM Units may attribute the same Shared Case.
_Avoid_: Case ownership, Case Unit field

**Unit Leadership**:
An MSM-owned association between an MSM Unit and a Shared Person. A Shared Person may lead or represent several MSM Units.
_Avoid_: Person ownership, Person Unit field

**Shared Case**:
A systemwide Case made available to one or more website channels. MSM may present it without owning or modifying it.
_Avoid_: MSM Case copy

**Shared Person**:
A systemwide Person who may be presented by one or more website channels. MSM may reference the Person without owning or modifying them.
_Avoid_: MSM Person copy

Website editions can intentionally override the presentation of shared Cases and People. “Without owning or modifying” above describes Unit attribution: selecting a Unit’s cases/leaders does not write relationships back to those shared documents. It does not prohibit separately authorized edits to a website edition. Service ownership is documented in the [platform guide](../../docs/SERVICE_CONTENT.md).

**Unit Page**:
The public page for one MSM Unit, combining its expertise, leadership, attributed Cases, and contact path.
_Avoid_: Unit profile card
